## GST Slabs for the Textile and Apparel Sector
The clothing and textile industry is a major economic driver in India. Under GST, the rate structure has undergone multiple revisions to simplify compliance and prevent tax leaks. Apparel and footwear tax rates are historically split based on the sale value of the items.

## 1. Apparels and Fabrics Slabs
The tax slabs for garments are defined as follows:
*   **Value-Based Slabs:** Previously, clothes costing below ₹1,000 attracted 5% GST, and those above attracted 12%. The GST Council rationalized the rates to simplify billing structures.
*   **Standard Slabs:** Currently, most readymade garments, apparel products, and fabrics attract a flat **12% GST** slab rate.
*   **Yarn and Fiber:** Cotton yarn attracts **5% GST**, while synthetic fibers and yarns are taxed at **12% GST**.

---

## 2. Footwear Slabs
Similar to clothing, footwear tax rates are aligned:
*   **Footwear under ₹1,000:** Attracts **5% GST** to keep essential footwear affordable for low-income brackets.
*   **Footwear above ₹1,000:** Attracts **12% GST** (standard leather boots, designer shoes, sports trainers).
