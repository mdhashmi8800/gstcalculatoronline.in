## How GST Applies to Gold and Jewelry Purchases
Gold has deep cultural and investment value in India. Under the GST regime, gold transactions attract a specialized, non-standard tax structure separate from the primary 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28% slabs. If you purchase gold jewelry, the invoice contains multiple tax lines that you must check for accuracy.

## The Dual-Layer Tax Structure on Jewelry
Gold jewelry purchases involve two distinct tax components:
1.  **Gold Metal Value:** The gold content (determined by purity/karat weight and market price) is taxed at a special flat rate of **3% GST**.
2.  **Jewelry Making Charges:** The service charge added by the jeweler for craftsmanship is taxed as a service at **5% GST**.

### Gold Tax Billing Example:
If you purchase gold jewelry where the raw gold value is ₹50,000 and the making charges are ₹5,000:
*   **Gold GST (3% of ₹50,000):** ₹1,500
*   **Making Charge GST (5% of ₹5,000):** ₹250
*   **Total Tax Paid:** ₹1,750
*   **Grand Total Invoice:** ₹56,750
*   *For local purchases:* Split the gold tax into 1.5% CGST and 1.5% SGST. Split the making charges GST into 2.5% CGST and 2.5% SGST.
