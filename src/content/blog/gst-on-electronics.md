## GST on Electronics: Complete Rate Guide for India (2026)

Electronics is one of the largest categories in India's GST framework, spanning everything from essential items like mobile phones to luxury appliances like large-screen televisions and air conditioners. The GST rates on electronics vary significantly — from 12% for smartphones to 28% for air conditioners and large-screen TVs.

This guide covers the current GST rates for all major electronics categories in India, with HSN codes, ITC rules, and practical billing examples.

## Detailed Analysis by Category

### 1. Mobile Phones — 12% GST (HSN 8517)

Mobile phones are one of the few electronics categories taxed at the lower 12% slab. This rate was increased from 0% to 12% in 2020 to discourage mobile phone imports and encourage domestic manufacturing under the PLI scheme.

**Billing Example — Selling a ₹25,000 Smartphone:**

| Component | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Base Price | — | ₹25,000 |
| CGST (6%) | ₹25,000 × 6% | ₹1,500 |
| SGST (6%) | ₹25,000 × 6% | ₹1,500 |
| **Invoice Total** | — | **₹28,000** |

> **Mobile accessories** (cases, screen protectors) attract 18% GST under HSN 8517 or 3926.

### 2. Laptops and Computers — 18% GST (HSN 8471)

Laptops are taxed at 18%, making them one of the most significant ITC opportunities for businesses. A company buying 10 laptops at ₹50,000 each pays ₹9,000 GST per laptop — ₹90,000 total ITC that can offset output tax liability.

**Billing Example — IT Company Buying Laptops (B2B):**

| Component | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Price per laptop | — | ₹50,000 |
| CGST (9%) | ₹50,000 × 9% | ₹4,500 |
| SGST (9%) | ₹50,000 × 9% | ₹4,500 |
| **Invoice per laptop** | — | ₹59,000 |
| **ITC claimable (per laptop)** | CGST + SGST | **₹9,000** |

### 3. Televisions — 18% (≤32") or 28% (>32")

Television GST rate depends on screen size:
- **Up to 32 inches:** 18% GST (HSN 8528)
- **Above 32 inches:** 28% GST (HSN 8528)

**Example — 55" Smart TV priced at ₹60,000:**

| Component | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Base Price | — | ₹60,000 |
| CGST (14%) | ₹60,000 × 14% | ₹8,400 |
| SGST (14%) | ₹60,000 × 14% | ₹8,400 |
| **Invoice Total** | — | **₹76,800** |

### 4. Air Conditioners — 28% GST (HSN 8415)

ACs fall in the luxury/sin goods category at 28%, making them the most heavily taxed common household appliance.

**Example — 1.5 Ton Split AC at ₹40,000:**

| Component | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Base Price | — | ₹40,000 |
| CGST (14%) | ₹40,000 × 14% | ₹5,600 |
| SGST (14%) | ₹40,000 × 14% | ₹5,600 |
| **Invoice Total** | — | **₹51,200** |

## ITC Rules for Electronics in Businesses

Businesses registered under GST can claim Input Tax Credit on electronics used for business purposes:

### Eligible ITC Claims
- **Office computers, laptops:** Full ITC claimable (business use)
- **CCTV cameras, surveillance systems:** ITC claimable (security)
- **Office printers, scanners:** ITC claimable
- **Mobile phones (business SIM):** ITC claimable for business-only phones

### Non-Eligible ITC Claims
- Electronics for **personal use** (not business)
- Consumer electronics purchased as **gifts for employees** (partial restrictions)
- Electronics used for **blocked credits** under Section 17(5)

## E-Commerce Sales of Electronics: GST Implications

Online platforms like Amazon, Flipkart, Croma, and Reliance Digital are required to **collect Tax at Source (TCS)** at 1% on behalf of sellers registered on their platform:

- Sellers receive payment net of 1% TCS
- TCS is reflected in their electronic cash ledger
- Sellers can adjust TCS against their GSTR-3B output tax liability

---

## Reverse Charge on Import of Electronics

Businesses importing electronics (phones, servers, equipment) must pay:
- **Basic Customs Duty (BCD):** Varies by product (typically 10–20%)
- **Social Welfare Surcharge:** 10% of BCD
- **IGST:** As applicable (12% for phones, 18% for laptops)
- **IGST is claimable as ITC** by the importer (unlike customs duty)

## Key Takeaways

1. **Mobile phones: 12% GST** — lowest among major electronics
2. **Laptops, TVs (≤32"), refrigerators: 18% GST** — standard electronics slab
3. **ACs, washing machines, TVs (>32%): 28% GST** — luxury category
4. **All B2B electronics purchases** allow ITC claims
5. **E-commerce sellers** face 1% TCS deduction by platform operators
