## GST Tax Slabs for the Electronic Industry
The electronic goods sector attracts substantial tax volumes in India. Slabs are split based on utility classification. While standard business equipment (like laptops and office monitors) is kept in the 18% slab to facilitate business credits, luxury consumer goods are placed in the highest 28% slab.

## 1. Electronics in the 18% GST Slab
The 18% slab applies to most standard business electronics and daily communication equipment:
*   **Mobile Phones:** Pre-configured at 18% to correct inverted duty tax issues.
*   **Computers & Laptops:** Desktops, notebook laptops, tablets, and servers.
*   **Peripherals:** Printers, keyboard units, mouse controllers, and monitors.
*   **Security Systems:** CCTV cameras, alarm systems, and recorders.

---

## 2. Electronics in the 28% GST Slab
Luxury home appliances and entertainment goods attract the highest 28% slab rate:
*   **Home Comfort:** Air conditioners (AC), refrigerators, and water heaters.
*   **Appliances:** Dishwashers, washing machines, and heavy food processors.
*   **Entertainment:** Large-screen television sets (above specific display sizes).
