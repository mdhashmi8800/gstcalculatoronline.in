## GST Rules for Freelancers and Independent Consultants
Freelancing, software development, copywriting, and independent consulting have grown massively in India. With these micro-businesses, understanding tax liabilities under the GST framework is necessary to prevent interest penalties. Freelancers are classified as service providers, meaning they fall under the standard service tax rules.

## 1. Registration Threshold Limits
Unlike traders who have a threshold limit of ₹40 lakh, freelancers and service providers must register for GST under the following conditions:
*   **National Threshold:** Aggregate annual turnover exceeds **₹20 Lakh** in a financial year.
*   **Special Category States:** Aggregate turnover exceeds **₹10 Lakh** in states like Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, and Tripura.

*Note:* If you supply services to clients within your own state, you charge CGST + SGST. If you serve clients in other states, it is an interstate supply and you charge IGST.

---

## 2. Zero-Rated Export Supplies (Foreign Clients)
If you export services to international clients (e.g., developers or designers serving clients in the USA, Europe, or Australia), the transaction is classified as a **Zero-Rated Supply** (0% GST).

To supply services without paying GST:
1.  **Export under LUT (Letter of Undertaking):** You file an online LUT on the GST portal at the start of the financial year. This allows you to invoice foreign clients with 0% tax.
2.  **Claim Refunds:** If you pay GST on business purchases (like laptops or internet connections), you can claim a refund of that accumulated Input Tax Credit (ITC) using our GST Refund Calculator.
