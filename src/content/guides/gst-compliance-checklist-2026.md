Maintaining 100% error-free GST compliance is vital for every registered taxpayer in India to avoid heavy penalties, interest charges, and blocking of Input Tax Credit (ITC).

## Essential Monthly Compliance Tasks

1. **GSTR-1 Outward Supply Reporting**: File by the 11th of every month for monthly filers.
2. **GSTR-2B ITC Auto-Drafted Statement**: Download on the 14th of every month to reconcile purchase invoices.
3. **GSTR-3B Monthly Return & Tax Payment**: File by the 20th of every month to clear net tax liabilities.
4. **E-Way Bill Verification**: Ensure all inter-state movements of goods exceeding ₹50,000 have valid active E-Way bills.
