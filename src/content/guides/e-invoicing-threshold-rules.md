The E-Invoicing system under Goods and Services Tax (GST) mandates electronic generation and reporting of B2B tax invoices directly to the Invoice Registration Portal (IRP).

## Key Components of an E-Invoice

- **IRN (Invoice Reference Number)**: A unique 64-character hash value.
- **Signed QR Code**: Printed on the physical or electronic invoice copy for quick verification by tax officers.
- **Auto-population**: E-invoice details automatically flow into GSTR-1 and E-Way Bill portals.
